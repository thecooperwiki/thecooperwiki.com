## Legacy Curricula

This wiki will maintain the curricula and course pages for the most recent catalog year.
As of writing this page, that is for Fall 2026 and onwards.

For those who enrolled prior to Fall 2026, please refer to the archives on Cooper's 
[official website](https://cooper.edu/students/registrar/course-catalog).

## Calculus Placement

The curricula on this wiki assume that students will enroll directly into Calculus I.

Students who pass the [placement exam](/incoming/calculus-placement) will begin with Calculus II in the fall semester of their freshman year.
