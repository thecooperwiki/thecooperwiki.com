**Credits**: 3

**Prerequisites**: [CS 394](/academics/courses/engineering/cs394)

> CS395 and CS396 constitute the year-long senior design project.  Students work in small groups on projects chosen with the advice and consent of the faculty advisor.  Projects may be oriented towards research or product development, and may be in any area of computer science. Students perform all aspects of project management, such as scheduling, budgeting, system design and developing milestones, as well as technical work including testing and performance evaluation.  Agile development methods are used. Students also give several spontaneous and rehearsed oral presentations, and prepare written reports.  Students attend weekly lectures covering:  social, economic, legal and ethical issues;  security;  design methodologies;  technical writing;  preparation of multimedia presentations and tailoring presentations to target audiences.
