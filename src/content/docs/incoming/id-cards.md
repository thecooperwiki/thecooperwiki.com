---
title: ID Cards
---

Students have to submit their own ID photos to ids@cooper.edu before Welcome Week begins.
You need an ID card to enter the building, and they will be given to you during orientation.

## Requirements
- Uniform and light background
- Head and shoulders visible
- Only you in the photo
- Hats, sunglasses, jewelry, etc not allowed
    - Normal glasses allowed
- File size less than 100 Kb
- File name format should be `firstname-lastname.jpg`
