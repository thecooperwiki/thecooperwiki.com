---
title: Health Forms
---

All incoming students need to fill out the health forms packet.
The completed forms can be submitted online or through the mail.

The forms can be found at [this link](https://cooper.edu/students/student-affairs/health/health-forms).

:::caution
Submitting forms late will impose a $100 fee. 
Some of the forms must be filled out by a licensed physician.
Make sure to book an appointment with your physician in time.
:::
