---
title: Health Insurance
---

Health insurance is required for all students.
Students without coverage will be billed for an insurance plan.
Additionally, international and exchange students are required to enroll in Cooper's insurance plan.
The insurance provided is [Wellfleet Student Insurance](https://www.studentinsurance.com/Client/566).

## Waiving Insurance

If you already have coverage, you can waive the insurance payment on Wellfleet's website.
A photo / PDF of your insurance card must be submitted as proof of insurance.
If you waive your insurance after already having paid the bill, the sum will be returned after the waiver is confirmed.
