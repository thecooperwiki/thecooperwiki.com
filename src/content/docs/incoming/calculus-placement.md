title: Calculus I Placement Exam
sidebar:
    label: Calc I Placement
---

Before the fall semester begins, incoming freshmen in the engineering school can sign up for a placement exam to skip Calculus I.
Official scores must be sent through CollegeBoard after signing up through a form on a student portal.
The exam is held in person during Welcome Week and is 3 hours long.

:::note
Students who pass will continue to Calculus II in the first semester, while students who fail will be in Calculus I.
:::
